# Submission Guide — ServiceNow Integrations Masterclass Final Exam

Follow these steps precisely so your instructor can review your work quickly and fairly.

## Before you record

1. **Confirm Exam Navigator membership.** Grading requires an active [Exam Navigator (or higher) membership](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf7pje1x5iZkkEF-Y3PLSA/join).
2. **Have your PDI ready.** Log in, elevate to admin if needed.
3. **Read all 3 exam pages first.** Plan the tasks; know the rubric.
4. **Practice once, silent, then record.** You have 5 minutes per video — plan is everything.

## Recording

* Use any screen recorder (Loom, QuickTime, OBS, Zoom). The tool doesn't matter, only the result.
* Record your **screen + microphone**. Camera is optional.
* Speak naturally: what you're doing, why, and what to look at.
* Keep each video to **5 minutes maximum**. Over that and it won't be graded.
* Export as MP4 or MOV.

## Uploading

1. Upload each video to **Google Drive**.
2. Right-click → **Share** → set to *Anyone with the link can view*.
3. Copy the shareable link for each video.

## Submitting

* Submit **all 3 exam videos together** in a single form entry — not one at a time.
* Include:
  * Your **name** and **email**
  * Your **YouTube channel handle** tied to your Exam Navigator membership
  * A link to **each of your 3 exam videos**
  * Any notes for the grader (optional)

The submission form link will be posted on the course home page.

## What happens next

* Submissions from active members are reviewed within **5–7 days**.
* You receive **written rubric feedback** for each exam plus your total score.
* On passing (70+), you receive your **branded Certificate of Completion**.
* Below 70 on any exam? **Resubmit for free** — as many times as needed.
