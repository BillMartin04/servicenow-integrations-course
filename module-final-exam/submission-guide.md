# Submission Guide

## Before you record

Complete and test your work before recording. Read all three exam pages and prepare a short demonstration against each evidence checklist.

Use only synthetic data and your own practice environment. Hide passwords, tokens, client secrets, connection credentials and any personal information before sharing a screen.

## Recording

Record your screen with voice narration and explain both the result and why it matters. Each of the three recordings must be no longer than five minutes; a camera is optional.

## Uploading

Keep the recordings private while preparing them. Before creating a viewable sharing link, review the entire recording for secrets and private data, then test the link's viewer access.

## Submitting

{% hint style="warning" %}
**Submission intake is not configured in this repository yet.** No verified submission form URL is available here. Prepare your evidence, but do not submit to an unrelated course form or post membership details publicly.
{% endhint %}

Once the course submission form is supplied, submit all three recordings together. Personal review requires active [Exam Navigator membership or a higher tier](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCMf7pje1x5iZkkEF-Y3PLSA/join); membership verification details belong only in the approved private submission process.

## What happens next

Personal review uses the published rubrics. The combined pass threshold is 70 out of 100, with 90 or above earning Distinction; an individual exam has a maximum of 33 or 34 points, not 100.

Use feedback to revise your evidence and resubmit as needed. Review timing and the submission destination will be provided when intake is configured.
