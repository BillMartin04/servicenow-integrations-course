### A Complete Hands-On Course by TechTalk with Bill

Welcome to the complete, hands-on ServiceNow Integrations Masterclass. Across **8 modules** and **26 video lessons**, you will go from the basics to production-ready integrations and REST APIs in ServiceNow — building everything yourself in a free Personal Developer Instance (PDI).

## What you will learn

1. Set up and configure your PDI for integration practice
2. Understand REST and SOAP fundamentals in a ServiceNow context
3. Design and build outbound and inbound REST integrations
4. Use Script Includes and GlideAjax to bridge client and server
5. Apply advanced integration patterns for reliability and scale
6. Manage your work with source control and modern tooling
7. Prove your skills with a graded portfolio video demo

## Who this course is for

* ServiceNow administrators and developers who want to master integrations and REST APIs
* Technical consultants preparing customer solutions
* Anyone building a portfolio of ServiceNow work for interviews or NIW/Global Talent evidence
